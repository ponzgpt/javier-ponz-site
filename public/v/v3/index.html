01 · NOW

Open agents. Two Hermes plugins.

Psychopomp and Sabbath, built for the Nous Research plugin hackathon, tested end to end on my own Hermes, in Desktop and Telegram, and merged into the official Hermes plugin catalog.

☤ Hermes plugin · in the official catalog

Psychopomp

Every week Hermes helps you write a page of your life. If you ever go silent for good, it carries your words to the people you love, but only after your witness sends a code. Named after Hermes's other job: guide of souls. Tested on my own Hermes in Desktop and Telegram, entered in the Nous Research plugin hackathon, and merged into the official Hermes plugin catalog on 11 November 2026 after maintainer review.

vibe-coded: my spec and tests, agent-written

☾ Hermes plugin · in the official catalog

Sabbath

Your agent never rests. Now it rests with you. Once a week Hermes says goodnight on your phone, pauses its own scheduled jobs, keeps you company in anything but work and sets the rest aside. When the day ends you get one gentle digest. And if the ox falls into the well: /sabbath ox. Merged into the official Hermes plugin catalog on 11 November 2026 after maintainer review.

04 · STORY

Ten years at the Genius Bar, then agents.

Apple first, because it's the strongest evidence. Then the move into AI, and what I'm studying while I make it.

  1. Sep 2014 – Feb 2018

    Specialist, then Technical Specialist · Apple Retail

    Parquesur, Madrid. Foundations in diagnosis, consultative support and daily execution under pressure.

    What the role involved
    • Foundations in diagnosis, consultative support and execution under pressure.
  2. Mar 2018 – Aug 2024

    Genius · Apple Retail

    Parquesur, Leganés. Six and a half years as the technical baseline of the store: advanced diagnosis on the cases nobody else had resolved, translating them for people with very different levels of understanding, and bringing newer technicians onto the bench.

    What the role involved
    • Diagnosed and repaired Mac, iPhone and iPad hardware and software faults, owning each case through post-repair checks; repair times among the shortest on the team; NPS stable and at or above the team average.
    • Colleagues used my case notes as the example to follow; kept case documentation to Apple's guidelines. Proposed repair-room process fixes to store leadership and linked Support with the stockroom on dead-on-arrival parts.
    • Mentored newer technicians and new Geniuses through shadowing and case feedback.
  3. Mar – Jun 2020

    AppleCare Support Advisor · Apple

    Remote, during COVID, as a volunteer. Concurrent cases under structured troubleshooting and documentation standards, working without a floor around me.

    What the role involved
    • Remote support: Tier 1 by phone, email and chat, concurrent cases under structured troubleshooting and documentation standards.
    • Escalated what Tier 1 could not resolve: documented the case, briefed Tier 2 first, then a live three-way handoff with the customer.
  4. Feb – Aug 2023 · Feb – Aug 2024

    Lead In-Store Experience · Apple Retail (secondment, twice)

    Xanadú first, then Parquesur. Two seven-month leadership secondments taken while holding the Genius role: planning, resourcing, events, coordination and the situations that fit no procedure. Led by removing obstacles, then went back to the bench.

    What the role involved
    • From the bench into leadership, twice: seven-month secondments taken while holding the Genius role. Worked alongside the management team as the link between them and the floor, running the store from opening to close: staffing and team breaks, customer flow, logistics.
    • Trained technical and sales staff, ran weekly cross-team alignment and co-owned scheduling optimisation; handled what no procedure covered.
  5. Aug 2024

    Aberdeen, Scotland

    Moved to the UK and started a deliberate transition into AI and full stack development.

  6. Aug 2024 – now

    AI, agent tooling, and building in public

    Studying AI and full stack at Universidad Rey Juan Carlos via Racks University, building Machines Do It Better, writing a newsletter (technoirNews, later Machines Do It Better; paused), and shipping small products that reach production.

  7. Nov 2026

    Two Hermes plugins · Nous Research hackathon

    Psychopomp and Sabbath, tested end to end on my own Hermes and merged into the official Hermes plugin catalog on 11 November after maintainer review; 31 tests between them.
