      Main project · work in progress

      MDIB · Machines Do It Better

      “You can't paste your clients' data into ChatGPT.”

      MDIB is where I put it all to work: private AI agents for professionals who handle other people's confidential information, each fitted to one person's work and owned by them. Built on Hermes Agent: the account, the data and the choice of model stay with the person who uses it. The Genius Bar reflex, applied to agents: get it working, explain it, leave you in control.
